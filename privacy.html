2. What Data We Process

2.1 Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit to our servers:

  • Your name, address, telephone number, or other directly identifying information (unless you create an Evigrade account for Pro features)
  • The URLs of the pages you visit, or a history of your browsing
  • Your medical history, diagnoses, or healthcare provider information (the pregnancy / lactation toggle is covered in sections 2.3 and 4)
  • Content of the lists you create within the extension (allergens, personal medications) – these remain on your device

2.2 Data processed locally on your device

The following data is stored using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API and remains on your device (Chrome may sync it across your signed-in devices, governed by Google's privacy policy):

  • Language preference
  • Region preference (if you choose one)
  • Acceptance of Terms of Service and Privacy Policy with timestamp and version
  • Subscription tier (Free / Pro / Professional), if you have an Evigrade account
  • Personal allergen and intolerance list (Pro feature, opt-in)
  • Pregnancy / lactation status toggle (Pro feature, opt-in). It stays on your device while you use the extension anonymously; if you sign in and enable cross-device sync, a copy is stored on our servers – see section 2.3
  • Personal medication list for the Pro Drug Interaction Reference (Pro feature, opt-in) – processed locally only

2.3 Data processed on our servers (only if you create an Evigrade account)

  • Email address (for account authentication)
  • Payment processor customer identifier (YooKassa, Stripe, or other; we do not store payment card details)
  • Pregnancy / lactation flags, only if you sign in and turn on cross-device sync. Stored under explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)) together with the version of the consent text. You can delete the server-side copy in one click from your account page
  • Subscription status and history

2.4 Analytics

The extension may collect anonymous usage statistics: activations, clicks per interface element, and the catalog entry that was matched on the page you opened (for example, «ashwagandha»). This tells us which substances people actually look up, so we know what to add to the catalog next. It is tied to a random installation identifier, never to your name or account, and it does not include page URLs or your browsing history. You can opt out in Settings.

Once per day, while your browser is running, the extension also sends a signal that this installation is still active. It carries the same random installation identifier, the extension version and your interface language, and nothing else. It lets us tell an active installation from a removed one without tracking what you do. Opting out in Settings stops this signal too. Events that fail to send are kept on your device for up to 30 days and retried, then discarded.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) – processing account data and subscription information necessary to provide Pro features
  • Explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) – optional analytics; opt-in toggles for pregnancy, lactation, allergen list
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) – security, abuse prevention, technical operation of the extension
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) – record-keeping for tax and consumer protection purposes

4. Special Category Data (GDPR Art. 9)

Information about pregnancy, lactation, allergens, and personal medication lists constitutes special category data concerning health under GDPR Art. 9. We process this data based on:

  • Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) – you must affirmatively enable each relevant toggle before such data is recorded
  • The allergen list and the personal medication list are stored only locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers
  • The pregnancy and lactation toggles also stay on your device while you use the extension anonymously. If you sign in and turn on cross-device sync, a copy is stored on our servers under the same explicit consent, together with the version of the consent text. You can delete the server-side copy in one click from your account page (right to erasure)
  • The Pro Drug Interaction Reference processes your medication list locally; the list itself is not sent to our backend

5. Data Retention

  • Locally stored data: retained until you uninstall the extension or manually clear it
  • Account data: retained while your account is active plus six (6) years after closure for tax and accounting compliance, as required by applicable law
  • Acceptance log (Terms / Privacy): retained while the extension is installed; required for evidence of consent under GDPR Art. 7(1)

8. Your Rights (GDPR Art. 15–22)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access (Art. 15) – obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification (Art. 16) – correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure (Art. 17) – request deletion of your data
  • Restriction (Art. 18) – limit processing
  • Portability (Art. 20) – receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object (Art. 21) – object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) – at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Not be subject to automated decision-making (Art. 22) – we do not engage in such processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at gankova.vik@gmail.com. We will respond within thirty (30) days as required by Art. 12(3).

9. Right to Lodge a Complaint (GDPR Art. 77)

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your member state of residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement. For Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD). A directory of EU supervisory authorities is maintained by the European Data Protection Board.

10. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to know, the right to delete, the right to correct, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by these laws. To exercise your rights, contact gankova.vik@gmail.com.

11. Children

Evigrade Meds is not directed at children under the age of sixteen (16) in the European Economic Area or under the age of thirteen (13) in the United States. We do not knowingly collect data from such individuals. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We will provide notice of material changes to this Privacy Policy at least thirty (30) days before they take effect, via the extension's update notification and a banner on the welcome screen. For material changes affecting your rights, re-acceptance may be required to continue using the extension.
