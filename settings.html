Evigrade Meds v0.1.0

Interface language

Language used for the popup, welcome screen, and notices.

Adverse event reporting region

Which national pharmacovigilance authority to link when you tap "Report side effect".

Special status

Used to show pregnancy or breastfeeding sections inside the product card. Stored locally on your device; never sent to our servers.

Anonymous usage analytics

Anonymous events (install, popup open, page scan, match found, language switch) are sent to evigrade.com with a random per-install identifier. Pages you visit, the products you look up, and your medication list are never transmitted. You can turn this off any time.

Reset legal acceptance

Re-displays the welcome screen and resets your acceptance record. Used for testing or if you want to review the terms again.

Clear all local data

Removes language, region, allergens, and any other data stored on this device. You will need to re-accept the welcome notice.
