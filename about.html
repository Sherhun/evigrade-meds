Sources

All information in Evigrade Meds is cited from the regulatory and scientific sources listed below. Each card displays the specific source and the date the information was last updated.

Regulatory authorities

  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) – DailyMed, Orange Book, OTC Monographs, Pregnancy and Lactation Labeling Rule (PLLR)
  • European Medicines Agency (EMA) – European Public Assessment Reports (EPAR)
  • Agencia Española de Medicamentos y Productos Sanitarios (AEMPS) – CIMA database, Spanish Summary of Product Characteristics (Ficha técnica)
  • UK Medicines and Healthcare Products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) – drug labels and product information
  • Ministry of Health of the Russian Federation (ГРЛС) – Государственный реестр лекарственных средств
  • World Health Organization (WHO) – WHO Model List of Essential Medicines, International Nonproprietary Names (INN)

Evidence synthesis and clinical guidelines

  • Cochrane Library – systematic reviews and meta-analyses
  • National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) – clinical guidelines (UK)
  • U.S. Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) – preventive care recommendations
  • British National Formulary (BNF)
  • Professional society guidelines: European Society of Cardiology (ESC), American Heart Association (AHA), European Alliance of Associations for Rheumatology (EULAR), American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO), and others

Dietary supplements and breastfeeding safety

  • NIH Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS)
  • LactMed – Drugs and Lactation Database (NIH)
  • e-lactancia.org – Asociación para la Promoción e Investigación Científica y Cultural de la Lactancia Materna (APILAM)
  • Hale's Medications and Mothers' Milk – Dr. Thomas W. Hale

Drug interactions

  • DDInter – Drug-Drug Interactions database
  • DrugBank (where licensed)
  • DailyMed FDA labels (Drug Interactions sections)
  • AEMPS Ficha técnica (Interacciones sections)

Adverse event reporting

  • FDA MedWatch (United States)
  • EudraVigilance (European Union)
  • AEMPS NotificaRAM (Spain)
  • MHRA Yellow Card (United Kingdom)
  • Росздравнадзор (Russian Federation)

Update policy

Regulatory labelling is monitored on a rolling basis. We aim to refresh material information within thirty (30) days of a published change in any cited source. The Extension displays the "last updated" date on every card; for the most current information, always consult the linked primary source.
